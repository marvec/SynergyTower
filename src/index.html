SynergyTower: a pixel-art office career game

A free pixel-art browser game about climbing the corporate ladder: start as a new hire, beat 47 silly mini-games and get promoted floor by floor up a 40-floor tower until you become CEO.

  • 47 mini-games: spreadsheets, inbox triage, reply-all storms, expense reports, karaoke at the office party and more, plus holiday ones like a dinner buffet, a massage and a sandcastle, and chores at home like the laundry and the ironing
  • Floor-wide quests: track down a colleague for their 360 review or hunt the whiteboards for your lost password
  • An HR performance review gates every promotion; reach floor 40 and decide who stays as CEO
  • A daily stand-up favour with a streak bonus, random office events (fire drills, printer meltdowns, office cake, surprise audits), a secret business trip on floors 10 and 30 (a drive to a client's office where tasks pay double), a secret holiday on floor 20 (a tropical resort where easy activities pay in coffee), a night shift on floors 5, 15, 25 and 35 (the office empty and dark, the machines twice as fast), a home office day on floors 8, 18, 28 and 38 (chores in your own flat while your status stays green), a desk to decorate (every floor sells different knick-knacks and stationery) and 40 badges to collect
  • Keyboard, mouse and touch controls; works offline; free, no sign-up
