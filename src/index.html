SynergyTower: a pixel-art office career game

A free pixel-art browser game about climbing the corporate ladder: start as a new hire, beat 33 silly office mini-games and get promoted floor by floor up a 40-floor tower until you become CEO.

  • 33 office mini-games: spreadsheets, inbox triage, reply-all storms, expense reports, karaoke at the office party and more
  • Floor-wide quests: track down a colleague for their 360 review or hunt the whiteboards for your lost password
  • An HR performance review gates every promotion; reach floor 40 and decide who stays as CEO
  • Keyboard, mouse and touch controls; works offline; free, no sign-up
