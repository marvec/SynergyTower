▶ PLAY SYNERGYTOWER IN YOUR BROWSER

Free. No download, no sign-up. Runs on desktop, tablet and phone, and works offline once loaded.

You are a new hire at the bottom of a 40-floor corporate tower. Walk each floor, do the office chores as 48 short mini-games, earn paperclips (the office currency) and KPI stars, survive your HR performance review and take the elevator up, floor by floor, until you become CEO.

The daily grind

  • Meeting Buzzword Bingo, Reply-All Apocalypse, Fridge Tetris, Calendar Defender, Expense Fraud Detective, Elevator Pitch, Fix the Spreadsheet, Badge Tailgate and many more.
  • Every task costs coffee. Coffee refills while you are away.
  • Earn stars, level up, pay for your keycard and pass the HR review to be promoted.
  • On floor 40 you decide which colleagues stay.

Office life

  • A daily stand-up favour and a Daily Memo: the same challenge for every player each day, with a result to share.
  • Fire drills, printer meltdowns, cake in the kitchen and surprise audits.
  • A desk to decorate, 43 badges to collect, up to 100 careers per browser.
  • Poke the furniture: the fridge, the plants, the vending machine and the HR filing cabinet all have something to say.
  • A >_ terminal on every floor, for those who know.

Secrets

Some floors hide more than desks: a business trip by car, a night shift, a home office day, a lost folder in a dark archive, a Christmas party in December, a tropical holiday and a roof with a view. How to find them is up to you.

Controls

  • Arrow keys / WASD or click to walk, Space / Enter or click to interact, Esc for the menu.
  • Every mini-game supports keyboard and mouse. On phones and tablets, tap.

Commodore 64 version

The full career also runs on a real or emulated Commodore 64: 40 floors, 33 mini-games plus the HR review, in 6502 assembly. Download the ZIP below (8 D64 disk images). Boot synergy-1a.d64 with LOAD"*",8,1. It runs in VICE (x64sc) or on a C64 with a 1541-compatible drive, 1541 Ultimate or Pi1541. Tested on PAL hardware with an Ultimate II. SD2IEC is not supported. More on the C64 page.

About

Written by Claude (AI) for Martin Večeřa: the code, the pixel art (drawn in code) and the music (synthesised in the browser) are all made by the program itself, with no external assets. Vanilla JavaScript and Canvas 2D at 480×270. The in-game shop is a labelled DEMO: there are no real payments.
